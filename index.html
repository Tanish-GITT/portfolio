01 About

Systems, not demos.

I like problems where a language model is only half the answer and the other half is careful plumbing — retrieval, state, and workflows that keep running when nobody is watching.

I'm a developer finishing an MCA with an artificial-intelligence specialisation at Maharishi Dayanand University, Rohtak. Most of what I know came from building projects end to end: chunking and embedding documents so a model can answer from them, giving a chatbot memory that survives a restart, and stitching APIs together so a multi-step workflow completes on its own.

I work in Python, lean on the OpenAI and Groq APIs for inference, Pinecone for vector search, and n8n when orchestration is the real problem. I care about the unglamorous parts — the shape of the data, where a pipeline fails, and whether the thing is still working tomorrow.
